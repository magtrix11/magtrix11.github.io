# Project Context

## Title

**We Only Get the Wings**

## Form

An experimental moving-image work / moving painting. The planned complete film is approximately 3:30–5:00, but the first implementation must be only one 8–12 second motion study.

## Core proposition

**Metamorphosis without disappearance.**

This is not a clean death-and-rebirth narrative. The organism is continuous even when its form becomes difficult to recognize. Earlier matter must survive inside later states: thread becomes nerve, root, route, seam, data path, rib, or membrane; pigment becomes skin; a fragment of a face becomes architecture; a cage becomes a wing without becoming a literal butterfly.

The governing idea is:

> Nothing disappears. It becomes difficult to recognize.

The working visual thesis is:

> A digital organism with textile memory.

## Emotional register

Materially intimate, bodily, intelligent, archaeological, biological, slightly uncanny, richly colored, and alive. Digitalness is welcome and should be perceptible. It must feel chosen and authored, not like a generic software effect or an imitation of handmade work.
