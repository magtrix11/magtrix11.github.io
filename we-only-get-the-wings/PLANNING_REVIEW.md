## Tension resolved deliberately

`TREATMENT.md` §4 describes a base of bone/parchment with colour as "small concentrated intrusions." The supplied board, the prompt and `VISUAL_RULES.md` ask for its saturated chromatic intensity. `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` says to follow the board's visual evidence while preserving the treatment's meaning.

**Resolution:** a pale **cool** gesso ground (not beige), as the board's pins almost always sit on pale walls or paper. The organism itself is dense with saturated painted matter, so the saturation is concentrated in the body rather than spread across the frame. That keeps the treatment's "colour as biological event" logic.

The treatment's "overhead archive table" world, the hard cut from black, and the full mask sequence all belong to later scenes. The study covers only this arc: organism → thread → eye → first rib.
